# BERT Models for Amharic

This document provides a list of BERT-based models that support the Amharic language. The models listed include both multilingual variants and those optimized for African languages.

---

## 1. AfriBERTa

**Description:**  
AfriBERTa is a BERT model specifically trained on African languages. It is designed to capture the unique linguistic characteristics of African languages, including Amharic, and offers robust performance on various NLP tasks.

---

## 2. am-roberta (XLM-RoBERTa-large)

**Description:**  
am-roberta is based on the XLM-RoBERTa-large architecture. It is a powerful multilingual model that has shown strong performance in Amharic as well as many other languages. Its large capacity makes it well-suited for complex language understanding tasks.

---

## 3. Davlan/afro-xlmr-large

**Description:**  
Davlan/afro-xlmr-large is a variant of the XLM-RoBERTa-large model, fine-tuned or optimized for African languages. It provides competitive performance on Amharic NLP tasks and is an excellent choice when processing African language data.

---

## 4. bert-amharic (bert-base-multilingual-cased)

**Description:**  
bert-amharic leverages the widely used `bert-base-multilingual-cased` model, which supports over 100 languages, including Amharic. This model serves as a solid baseline for Amharic language processing, offering balanced performance across various tasks.

---
